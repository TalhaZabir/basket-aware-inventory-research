# P1 robustness analysis report

The alpha grid was extended to [0.0, 0.25, 0.5, 1.0, 1.5, 2.0, 3.0]; matched-inventory calibration selected alpha = 1.5, which is interior to the grid. The selection minimizes matched cost subject to at least a one-percentage-point complete-basket-fill improvement over alpha zero. The full calibration table is `alpha_extended_validation_calibration.csv`.

Bootstrap intervals resample the 14 observed test weeks with replacement and rerun the paired policy comparison under common lead-time draws: 100 resamples per scenario and 1,200 pooled draws per contrast. The pooled file reports quantiles over all draws; it does not average scenario-level interval endpoints, which would not be a valid interval for the pooled average. These intervals represent sampling uncertainty over the realized test-week composition; they do not turn synthetic inventory outcomes into observed inventory truth. Results are in `bootstrap_test_week_results.csv`, `bootstrap_test_week_summary.csv`, and `bootstrap_test_week_pooled_summary.csv`.

The rolling-origin check re-estimates the product universe, forecasts, and co-purchase scores using data through 2010-12-27, validates through 2011-02-28, and evaluates an earlier 2011-03-07 to 2011-06-06 test window. This current check contains one earlier window. Mean cost/inventory/item fill/complete-basket fill were 2,072,599/244,587/0.840/0.633 for P1, 2,104,885/274,204/0.861/0.679 for P2, 2,128,197/287,349/0.867/0.686 for P3, and 2,183,818/318,241/0.883/0.720 for P4. Results are in `rolling_origin_results.csv` and `rolling_origin_summary.csv`.

# Highlights

- A transaction-derived graph buffer is tested against popularity at matched simulated inventory.
- Across effectively 4 x 3 factorial settings, the graph does not improve service over popularity.
- A low-popularity, high-lift exploratory stratum is the conditional exception.
- The mechanism is allocation: popularity protects high-volume basket hubs first.
- Reproducible data preparation, simulation code, figures, and audit tables are released publicly.
